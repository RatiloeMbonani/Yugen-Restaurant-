Our Philosophy

Our Story

YŪGEN was founded with a vision to bring the art of Japanese dining to the heart of the city. Our journey began with a passion for authentic flavour, meticulous craftsmanship, and a commitment to an unforgettable dining experience.

From sourcing the finest ingredients to creating a serene and elegant atmosphere, every aspect of YŪGEN is designed to transport our guests to Japan. Our chefs blend traditional techniques with modern culinary artistry, ensuring each dish is a masterpiece.

At YŪGEN, dining is not just about food — it's about creating memories. We invite you to join us on this culinary journey and experience the essence of Japanese hospitality.

Meet the Chef

Chef Kenji Nakamura

With years of experience in traditional Japanese cuisine, Chef Kenji combines authentic technique with contemporary presentation to create a dining experience that is both elegant and memorable.

His passion for craftsmanship and dedication to premium ingredients are reflected in every plate served at YŪGEN.
